# AROYA Training Content Brand Guide

## Visual Identity

**Primary Font:** Arial (substitute for Inter in non-web contexts)
- Titles: Arial Bold, 28-36pt
- Subtitles: Arial Regular, 18-24pt
- Body: Arial Regular, 14-16pt
- Speaker notes: Arial Regular, 12pt

**Color Palette:**
- Primary: AROYA Magenta (#E91E63 or closest brand match)
- Secondary: Dark charcoal (#1A1A2E) for backgrounds on dark theme slides
- Accent: White (#FFFFFF) for text on dark backgrounds
- Text on light backgrounds: Charcoal (#333333)
- Success/positive: Green (#4CAF50)
- Warning/attention: Amber (#FFC107)
- Data visualization: Use magenta as primary chart color, charcoal as secondary

**Logo Usage:**
- AROYA logo top-left on every slide
- Minimum clear space: equal to the height of the "A" in AROYA
- Never stretch, recolor, or place on busy backgrounds
- Use white logo on dark backgrounds, dark logo on light backgrounds

## Slide Layout Standards

**Title Slide (per module):**
- Dark background (#1A1A2E)
- Module number and title in white, left-aligned
- Persona badge in top-right (Partner | Customer | Internal)
- AROYA logo bottom-left
- Magenta accent bar at bottom (4px)

**Content Slides:**
- Light background preferred for readability
- Maximum 5 bullet points per slide
- One key visual or chart per slide (never text-only for 3+ consecutive slides)
- Section dividers use dark background with magenta left-bar accent

**Chart/Data Slides:**
- Chart.js or equivalent styling
- Magenta as primary data color
- Grid lines: light gray (#E0E0E0)
- Labels: Arial 12pt charcoal

## Tone and Voice

**Overall:** Professional, confident, grower-first. AROYA is a tool that respects the craft of cultivation while adding data-driven precision.

**Words to use:** Visibility, precision, control, consistency, data-driven, root zone, real-time, actionable
**Words to avoid:** Revolutionary, disruptive, game-changing, easy (growers don't want easy, they want precise), cheap, basic

**Partner tone:** Peer-to-peer. "You'll be able to..."
**Customer tone:** Expert guide. "AROYA shows you..."
**Internal tone:** Coach. "When the grower says X, you say..."

## Narration Voice Direction (for ElevenLabs)

**Voice characteristics:**
- Male or female, American English, professional but not corporate
- Pace: moderate (140-160 words per minute)
- Tone: confident, knowledgeable, conversational (not reading a script)
- Avoid: monotone, overly enthusiastic, salesy inflection
- Model: think experienced cultivation consultant briefing a colleague, not a narrator reading a textbook

**Pronunciation notes:**
- VPD: spoken as "V-P-D" (three letters)
- EC: spoken as "E-C" (two letters)
- VWC: spoken as "V-W-C" (three letters)
- AROYA: spoken as "ah-ROY-ah" (three syllables, emphasis on second)
- Dryback: one word, emphasis on "dry"
- Substrate: "SUB-straight"

## File Naming Convention

All output files follow this pattern:
```
{persona}-module-{NN}-{slug}-{type}.md
```

Examples:
- partner-module-04-crop-steering-slides.md
- customer-module-04-crop-steering-narration.md
- internal-module-04-crop-steering-assessment.md
- partner-module-04-crop-steering-facilitator.md
