# AROYA Elite Partner Training Program

Channel Partner & Influencer Certification Curriculum

This document outlines a comprehensive training curriculum designed to equip channel partners, cultivation consultants, advisors, and influential growers with the knowledge required to demonstrate, explain, and successfully position AROYA technology in commercial cultivation environments. The program combines system proficiency, crop steering fundamentals, and a practical sales framework to create a scalable partner-driven revenue channel.

## Program Objectives

- Develop a trained network of cultivation professionals capable of advocating for AROYA solutions
- Teach crop steering principles and irrigation strategy so partners understand the cultivation impact
- Provide system proficiency enabling partners to confidently demonstrate the platform
- Train partners to identify operational problems and position AROYA as the solution
- Create a scalable partner ecosystem capable of generating significant new revenue

## Program Format

- Training Duration: 8 hours (in person)
- Format: Instructor-led with demonstrations, discussion, and scenario exercises
- Audience: Consultants, cultivation influencers, hydro retail advisors, nutrient technical teams, and grow managers
- Outcome: Participants receive AROYA Certified Partner status upon completion

## MODULE 1 -- Industry Context

**Objective:** Establish the economic and operational context driving adoption of precision cultivation tools.

**Topics Covered:**
- Price compression and margin pressure in commercial cultivation
- Increasing demand for consistent quality and yield
- Operational inefficiencies caused by guesswork irrigation
- Transition toward data-driven cultivation systems

**Key Message:** Most cultivation teams have limited visibility into the root zone. AROYA enables growers to understand how plants interact with their substrate in real time, allowing for informed irrigation decisions.

**Learning Outcome:** Partners can articulate the market forces creating demand for precision cultivation technology.

---

## MODULE 2 -- Technology Overview

**Objective:** Provide partners with a clear understanding of system components and what each device measures.

**Topics Covered:**
- Substrate sensing technology
- Environmental monitoring
- Irrigation data tracking
- Gateway networking architecture

**Measurements Explained:**

Substrate Metrics:
- Volumetric water content (VWC)
- Electrical conductivity (EC)
- Root zone temperature

Environmental Metrics:
- Air temperature
- Relative humidity
- Vapor pressure deficit (VPD)
- CO2 levels

**Learning Outcome:** Partners can confidently explain what the system measures and why those measurements matter to growers.

---

## MODULE 3 -- Platform Demonstration

**Objective:** Train partners to interpret cultivation data and explain system outputs.

**Topics Covered:**
- Dashboard overview
- Graph interpretation
- Irrigation event tracking
- Dryback curves
- EC stacking patterns

**Exercise:** Participants review sample graphs and practice explaining what the data means and what actions a grower might take.

**Learning Outcome:** Partners can walk a grower through the AROYA dashboard and explain what the data is telling them.

---

## MODULE 4 -- Crop Steering Fundamentals

**Objective:** Introduce the biological principles behind crop steering.

**Concepts Covered:**
- Relationship between irrigation strategy and plant morphology
- Vegetative vs generative steering
- Environmental influence on plant responses

**Vegetative Steering Strategy:**
- Frequent irrigation events
- Smaller drybacks
- Stable EC

**Generative Steering Strategy:**
- Larger drybacks
- Reduced irrigation frequency
- Elevated EC levels

**Learning Outcome:** Partners can explain the concept of crop steering and how AROYA enables growers to steer intentionally rather than accidentally.

---

## MODULE 5 -- Irrigation Strategy

**Objective:** Demonstrate how crop steering concepts translate into irrigation practices.

**Topics Covered:**
- Shot size calculations
- Shot timing
- Dryback targets
- Irrigation windows

**Phase Examples:**

- Vegetative Growth: Frequent irrigation, minimal drybacks
- Transition Phase: Delayed first irrigation, increased dryback percentage
- Bulk Flowering: Moderate shot size, EC stacking
- Late Flower: Reduced irrigation frequency, controlled stress to encourage maturation

**Learning Outcome:** Partners can discuss irrigation strategy changes across growth phases and explain why they matter.

---

## MODULE 6 -- Opportunity Identification

**Objective:** Teach partners how to identify cultivation facilities that would benefit from AROYA.

**Common Indicators:**
- Inconsistent yields
- Limited visibility into substrate conditions
- Manual or guesswork irrigation practices
- Facilities attempting to scale production

**Typical Grower Statements:**
- "We water the same every day."
- "We don't know our runoff EC."
- "We're trying to dial in irrigation."

**Learning Outcome:** Partners can recognize opportunity signals during facility visits and conversations.

---

## MODULE 7 -- Sales Discovery Process

**Objective:** Provide a simple framework for partner-led discovery conversations.

**Key Discovery Questions:**
- How many cultivation rooms do you operate?
- What irrigation strategy do you currently use?
- Do you monitor runoff EC?
- Do you track dryback percentages?

**Goal:** Identify operational challenges and establish the need for improved root zone visibility.

**Learning Outcome:** Partners can conduct a structured discovery conversation that surfaces AROYA-relevant pain points.

---

## MODULE 8 -- The AROYA Pitch

**Objective:** Equip partners with a clear, concise explanation of the system's value.

**Core Narrative:** Most growers lack visibility into what is happening in their root zone. This system provides real-time data showing how plants interact with their substrate, enabling growers to refine irrigation strategy and improve crop performance.

**Learning Outcome:** Partners can deliver a 60-second elevator pitch that resonates with growers.

---

## MODULE 9 -- Deal Strategy

**Objective:** Teach partners how to introduce AROYA through low-friction pilot deployments.

**Pilot Deployment:**
- Typical Setup: 4-6 substrate sensors + climate sensor + gateway
- Typical Cost Range: $4,000 - $7,000
- Purpose: Demonstrate value quickly and expand to additional rooms once results are proven

**Full Facility Deployment:**
- Typical Range: $20,000 - $100,000+ depending on facility size

**Learning Outcome:** Partners can structure a pilot proposal and articulate the expansion path.
